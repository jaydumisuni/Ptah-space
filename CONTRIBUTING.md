# Contributing to Ptah

Thank you for your interest in Ptah.

Ptah is currently in pre-release development. Contributions should improve the public source, documentation, tests, packaging, or supported integrations without introducing private data or material that cannot be redistributed.

## Before contributing

- Check existing issues and pull requests before starting overlapping work.
- Keep changes focused and explain the user-visible or technical problem being solved.
- Add or update tests when behaviour changes.
- Update public documentation when setup, usage, compatibility, or behaviour changes.
- Submit only material you own or are authorised to contribute.

## Licence

Unless clearly stated otherwise, an intentional contribution accepted into this repository is submitted under the Apache License, Version 2.0, consistent with section 5 of that licence.

Third-party material keeps its original licence and required notices. Do not copy source, assets, documentation, datasets, firmware, or binaries into Ptah unless redistribution and modification rights have been verified.

## Do not submit

Do not place any of the following in public issues, commits, pull requests, tests, or logs:

- passwords, tokens, private keys, signing keys, or credentials;
- personal, customer, employee, supplier, payment, or device-identifying data;
- private infrastructure details, endpoints, configuration, or operational records;
- proprietary or restricted third-party material;
- private research, internal procedures, or confidential product information.

Use the private reporting route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities or accidental exposure.

## Pull requests

A pull request should include:

1. a clear purpose;
2. the affected public behaviour or documentation;
3. testing or verification performed;
4. compatibility or migration notes where relevant;
5. third-party licence and attribution details where applicable.

Maintainers may request changes or decline contributions that create security, privacy, licensing, compatibility, or long-term maintenance problems.
